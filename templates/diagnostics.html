Edge Production SLA
Takeaway #1: Dual 0.50 Dropout Layers Overfitting Defense

Mitigates neuron co-adaptation across 129,600 flattened spatial parameters, preventing model hallucination during harsh facility lighting shifts or ambient CCTV backlighting.

Takeaway #2: Quantization Preservation FP32 → INT8

Post-training INT8 tensor quantization achieves a 4x reduction in weight footprint (6.88M parameters to 1.8MB) while introducing under 0.18% top-line accuracy degradation.

Takeaway #3: Ingress Invariant SLA < 25ms Real-time

Mean forward pass execution (16.4ms) comfortably outpaces the 25ms hardware solenoid strike threshold, preventing bottleneck congestion during high-throughput shift rotations.
